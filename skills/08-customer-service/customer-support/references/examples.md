# 客服回复示例

## 示例 1：产品质量投诉

### 输入

客户消息：
> "I bought this water fountain 2 weeks ago and it's already leaking from the bottom. My floor is damaged. This is unacceptable. I want a full refund."

订单信息：
- 订单号：112-1234567-1234567
- 产品：FlowPaw 智能饮水机
- 购买时间：2 周前
- 客户：美国

### 输出

```markdown
## 客服回复建议

### 问题分类
产品质量投诉 - 漏水

### 情绪判断
愤怒 / 不满

### 回复要点
1. 立即道歉并共情
2. 承认漏水问题
3. 提供排查步骤
4. 提供换货或退款选项（需人工确认）
5. 记录产品问题反馈给质量团队

### 推荐回复（英文）

Subject: We're truly sorry about the leaking fountain

Hi [Customer Name],

I'm so sorry to hear that your FlowPaw fountain started leaking after just two weeks—and that it damaged your floor. That's absolutely not the experience we want for you or your pet.

Let's get this sorted out right away. Could you please:
1. Check that the water tank is fully seated on the base
2. Confirm the rubber seal around the pump is in place
3. Send us a quick photo of where the leak is coming from

While we look into this, I'd like to offer you two options:
- **Replacement**: We'll ship a new unit to you today with expedited shipping
- **Full refund**: We'll process a complete refund once the unit is returned

Again, I'm really sorry for the trouble. Please reply with your preferred option and we'll take care of it immediately.

Best,
[Agent Name]
FlowPaw Customer Care

### 是否需要升级
是。涉及产品质量 + 财产损失，需人工客服确认退款金额和赔偿方案。

### 是否需要创建工单
是。创建质量反馈工单，追踪该批次漏水问题。
```

## 示例 2：物流查询

### 输入

客户消息：
> "Where is my order? I ordered 5 days ago and haven't received any tracking update."

订单信息：
- 订单号：112-7654321-7654321
- 物流状态：已发货，运输中
- 预计送达：3 天后

### 输出

```markdown
## 客服回复建议

### 问题分类
物流查询

### 情绪判断
焦虑 / 轻微不满

### 回复要点
1. 确认订单和感谢
2. 提供当前物流状态
3. 给出预计送达时间
4. 提供追踪链接
5. 承诺持续跟进

### 推荐回复（英文）

Hi [Customer Name],

Thanks for reaching out! I checked your order #112-7654321-7654321 and it was shipped on July 28th.

Current status: In transit
Estimated delivery: August 3rd
Tracking number: 1Z999AA10123456784
Track here: [link]

Please allow 1-2 more days for the carrier to update the tracking. If you don't see movement by August 2nd, just reply to this message and we'll follow up with the carrier right away.

Thanks for your patience!

Best,
[Agent Name]

### 是否需要升级
否。

### 是否需要创建工单
否。标准物流查询，直接回复即可。
```
